How to use Wordle Nerdles

Quick start
  1. Go to the Today page.
  2. Create an account with your display name, email, and password.
  3. Email confirmation is no longer required.
  4. After signing up or signing in, refresh the page once.
  5. Paste your Wordle share text into the submit form.
  6. Add a screenshot if you want. It is optional and you can add it later.
  7. Tap Preview parse if you want to double-check it, then tap Submit result.
  8. Your result will show up on the home page, archive, and leaderboard.
Tip: the copied Wordle share text is the official score for the leaderboard.

Step 1 — Find your way around

Navigation
  1. Today is where you sign in and submit your score.
  2. Leaderboard shows daily, weekly, monthly, and all-time rankings.
  3. Archive shows older puzzle boards and screenshots.
  4. Past Answers lets you browse older Wordle answers.
  5. Profile lets you edit your avatar and catchphrase.

Step 2 — Sign up or sign in

Account
  1. Enter a display name that your friends will recognize.
  2. Use your email and choose a password.
  3. Tap Sign up if it is your first time.
  4. Tap Sign in after your account already exists.
  5. Refresh the page once after signup or login. This helps the account state update correctly.

Step 3 — Submit your score

Submission
  1. Copy the normal Wordle share text from the game.
  2. Paste it into the Wordle share text box.
  3. Screenshot upload is optional.
  4. Use Preview parse to make sure the pasted result looks correct.
  5. Tap Submit result.
  6. If you forgot the screenshot, you can add it later under your submission.
